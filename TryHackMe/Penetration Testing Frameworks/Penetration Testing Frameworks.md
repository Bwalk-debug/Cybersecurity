# Objective

The objective of this room was to introduce the different penetration testing frameworks and methodologies used throughout the cybersecurity industry. It explained how penetration testers choose a framework based on an organization's objectives, scope, and security requirements to perform a structured and professional assessment.

---

# Key Concepts

## What is a Penetration Testing Framework?

A penetration testing framework is a structured methodology that guides penetration testers through a security assessment. It provides an organized process for planning, executing, documenting, and reporting findings while ensuring the assessment stays within the client's authorized scope.

Rather than using the same approach for every engagement, penetration testers select a framework that best aligns with the organization's needs and the goals of the assessment.

---

## Frameworks Covered

### PTES (Penetration Testing Execution Standard)

PTES provides a complete penetration testing methodology that covers the entire assessment process, from pre-engagement planning to reporting and post-engagement activities.

---

### OSSTMM (Open Source Security Testing Methodology Manual)

OSSTMM is a standardized methodology for evaluating operational security across networks, wireless systems, physical security, and human interactions.

---

### NIST

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) develops cybersecurity standards and best practices that organizations use to perform structured and repeatable security assessments.

---

### OWASP

OWASP focuses on web application security and provides guidance on identifying and mitigating common web vulnerabilities through resources such as the OWASP Top 10.

---

### MITRE ATT&CK

MITRE ATT&CK is a knowledge base of real-world adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs). It helps security professionals understand how attackers operate and how defenders can detect and respond to those techniques.

---

# Why Frameworks Are Important

Following a penetration testing framework provides a structured approach for conducting a security assessment. Frameworks help ensure that important steps are not overlooked, improve consistency, and keep the assessment aligned with the client's objectives.

Frameworks also help penetration testers remain within the authorized scope provided by the organization, ensuring the assessment is both professional and legally compliant.

---

# Scope Matters

One of the most important lessons from this room is that every penetration test has a defined scope. The organization determines which systems, applications, or networks are authorized for testing.

Penetration testers must always stay within the approved scope and choose a methodology that best fits the organization's goals and requirements.
